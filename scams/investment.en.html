Investments and “guaranteed returns”

You’re offered to invest in a “famous company project”, crypto or “exchange trading” with a guaranteed high return. The first payouts are made so you invest more.

Signs

  • Guaranteed returns: 20–30% a month or a week
  • Contact only via Telegram or WhatsApp, a “personal manager”
  • To withdraw, you must pay a “tax” or “fee”

What to do

  1. Don’t invest based on messenger offers or ads
  2. Check the company on the financial regulator’s website
  3. Don’t pay a “withdrawal fee” — you won’t get the money back that way
