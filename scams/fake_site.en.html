How to spot a fake website

A fake looks like the original, but the address is different. The key is the domain: the part before the first single slash. For Kaspi it’s kaspi.kz, for eGov it’s egov.kz.

Signs

  • Extra words in the address: kaspi-bonus, halyk-secure
  • Swapped letters: kaspl, ka5pi, rn instead of m
  • A foreign domain zone: .top, .xyz, .site, .click
  • The site immediately asks for a card, code or password

What to do

  1. Use bookmarks or type the address yourself
  2. Check the address in CheckLink: it compares the domain with official ones
  3. The HTTPS padlock doesn’t mean the site is honest — look at the domain
