A loan in your name

They call to say a loan is “being taken out” in your name and offer to “cancel” it with a counter-loan and a transfer to a “protected account”.

Signs

  • They suggest a loan to “cancel” another one
  • They ask for your ID number, ID card details or a code

What to do

  1. End the call and phone your bank
  2. Check your credit history through official services
  3. You can set a self-ban on loans via eGov
