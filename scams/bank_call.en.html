A call “from the bank” or “security department”

The caller claims to be from a bank, the police or the KNB, scares you with a “suspicious transaction” and asks for a code, card details, a transfer or an app install.

Signs

  • They call you and rush you
  • They know your name and part of your card number — that proves nothing
  • They ask for an SMS code, CVV, PIN or to install an app
  • They tell you not to tell anyone

What to do

  1. Hang up — it’s safe and polite enough
  2. Call the bank yourself using the number on your card or in the app
  3. Never share codes, PIN or CVV — a real bank never asks for them
  4. Tell older relatives about this scheme
