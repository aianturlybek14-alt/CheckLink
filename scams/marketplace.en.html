Buying and selling via classifieds

On OLX, Kolesa and Krisha scammers pose as buyers and send a “link to receive money”, or ask for prepayment for goods that don’t exist.

Signs

  • The “buyer” moves the chat to WhatsApp or Telegram
  • A “safe deal” link on a foreign domain
  • The seller wants full prepayment and rushes you

What to do

  1. Talk and pay only inside the platform
  2. Receiving money never requires a CVV or codes
  3. Check buyers’ links in CheckLink
