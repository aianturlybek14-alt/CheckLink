Scam schemes and how to spot them · RU · KK

“Jobs” that require you to pay

You’re offered easy side work: likes, reviews, “buying out goods”. At first they pay a little, then ask you to deposit money for “access”, “insurance” or a “level up”.

Signs

  • The employer asks you to pay them
  • Too much money for simple tasks

What to do

  1. Don’t send money to an “employer”
  2. Look for work on trusted platforms and check the company
