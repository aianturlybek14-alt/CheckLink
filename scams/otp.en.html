SMS and push codes are for you only

An SMS or push code confirms a login or a payment. Whoever gets it can enter your account or make a transaction in your name.

Signs

  • Someone asks for the code by phone or messenger
  • “The code was sent to you by mistake, forward it”
  • The SMS says “don’t share”, yet someone asks you to share it

What to do

  1. Never tell or forward codes to anyone
  2. Read the whole SMS: it says what the code is for
  3. If you already shared it, call your bank or change the password at once
