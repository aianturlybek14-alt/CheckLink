Hijacked WhatsApp and Telegram

Accounts are hijacked by tricking you into sharing the login code: “forward the code, it came by mistake”, “vote for my niece”, “confirm the login”. Then friends are asked for money in your name.

Signs

  • You’re asked to forward an SMS code or log in “with Telegram” on another site
  • A friend suddenly asks to borrow money in chat

What to do

  1. Turn on two-step verification: WhatsApp → Settings → Account; Telegram → Privacy → Cloud password
  2. Never forward login codes
  3. Verify money requests by calling a number you know
  4. Turn on messenger protection in CheckLink’s Shield
