Strong passwords

Short and personal passwords (a name, a birth date, qwerty) are guessed in seconds. One password everywhere means one leak opens everything.

Signs

  • The password contains a name or birth year
  • One password for email, bank and social media

What to do

  1. Use a phrase of 3–4 unrelated words plus a number
  2. A separate password for each service; a password manager helps
  3. You can test strength in CheckLink’s Passwords section
